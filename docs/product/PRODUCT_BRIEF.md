# Product brief: Brief

## Context
Inspired by a Philippine fresh graduate's exhausting and expensive job search: applicants lose track of postings, travel between interviews, repeat application entry, lack clarity on questionable offers, and struggle to prepare. Primary audience: first-time job hunters, students/new grads, career switchers and workers between jobs, including low-connectivity users.

## One-line pitch
**Save a job in seconds, stay organized, know what to ask, and rehearse the interview—even offline.**

## Jobs to be done
- Capture from a screenshot/poster, photo, link, or typed text; AI extracts details locally; always verify before saving.
- Track your personal job hunt independently of job marketplaces, with statuses and timeline.
- Discover external listings *online* via an API and save them locally for later offline analysis.
- Understand job descriptions, ask about employer red flags, clarify salary/requirements, compare to a locally stored resume/profile.
- Simulate relevant mock interviews privately using the saved job and optional resume, track progress and feedback.
- Remember interview deadlines/events and personal notes.

## Explicit non-goals / prohibitions
- No pretending to have submitted an application to an employer from within Brief.
- No invented live listings, interview details, scraped logos or salaries, or guaranteed job outcomes.
- No replacing local inference with online LLM inference, even if offline model installation is hard.
- No forced account, resume upload, paywall or online signup in MVP.
- No flashy 3D mascot or arms: mascot is the flat, black-outline, two-legged supplied white briefcase.
- No separate Events list page in application tracker; Calendar owns events, Applications owns applications.

## Metrics / demo
- Time to save a job from a screenshot (review included)
- Zero network calls for importing/scoring/responding after model installed
- Number of applications tracked, interviews practiced
- User success in adding and revising a job under airplane mode
- Transparent error states and user trust, not only animations.
